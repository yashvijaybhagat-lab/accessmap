The problem

Most maps don't tell you if you can get in the door.

A place can have hundreds of reviews and still say nothing about step-free access, door width, or whether the accessible restroom actually works. AccessMap gives you that information before you go.

1.3B people worldwide live with a significant disability.Source: World Health Organization, 2023

What it does

Everything you need to know before you go.

Accessibility map

See real places near you, each scored for mobility, hearing, vision and sensory access. Filter by what matters to you.

Step-free routes

Plan routes that avoid stairs and flag barriers along the way, tuned to your needs.

Live alerts

Know about broken elevators and blocked entrances from community reports and transit outage feeds.

AI-checked photos

Community photos of ramps, doors and restrooms are checked by AI, so you can trust what you see.

Made to fit you

High contrast, larger text, a readable font, reduced motion, larger tap targets and more, all saved on your device.

Community reviews

Read and share real experiences, rated across the same four areas of access.
